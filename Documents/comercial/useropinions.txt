I am putting this in instruments, but it could have gone into effects and maybe even hosts, too. 

I just bought VSTForx, because it is now available in 64 bit. It is everything that I loved about the chaining abilities in EnergyXT, but it also offers really interesting parameter modulations for every parameter in your plugin. I have created some really interesting fx chains out of plugins that I had been bored with. 

It is not completely intuitive, especially some of the parameter to processor chaining, but there is a very good manual included. The dev, Johannes is very responsive, too. Question were answered in under a couple of hours. 

Plus it was only $20. I have no connection to the dev, but I was just very impressed with the plugin and the dev himself.
